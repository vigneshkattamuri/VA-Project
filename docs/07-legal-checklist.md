# Statutory & Legal Verification Checklist: Vighnesh AgroCare

## Legal Disclaimer
> **IMPORTANT NOTICE**:
> This document does **not** state legal conclusions, provide binding statutory interpretations, or substitute for formal legal and tax counsel. Agrochemicals and pesticides are strictly regulated hazardous commodities in India under the Insecticides Act, 1968, the Insecticides Rules, 1971, and Andhra Pradesh state agricultural regulations.
>
> Every item in this checklist must be reviewed, confirmed, and signed off by the business owner in consultation with the **Department of Agriculture (Government of Andhra Pradesh)** and a licensed **Chartered Accountant** prior to commercial launch.

---

## Statutory Verification Checklist

| Item ID | Statutory Area & Regulatory Context | Verification Authority | Verification Status |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **L01** | **Insecticides & Pesticides Retail Sale Licence**: Validity of retail licence to sell, stock, or exhibit for sale insecticides under Section 13 of the Insecticides Act, 1968 and Rule 10 of the Insecticides Rules, 1971 in Andhra Pradesh. Verification of premises endorsement, qualified person certification, and valid principal supply certificates from manufacturers. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L02** | **Mandatory Display of Retail Licence**: Statutory requirement under Rule 10(1A) of the Insecticides Rules, 1971 to prominently display the insecticide retail licence on the shop premises, and verification of whether licence numbers must also be displayed across the digital PWA header, footer, and generated receipts. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L03** | **Mandatory Sale and Stock Records**: Compliance of digital inventory logs with Rule 15 of the Insecticides Rules, 1971, which mandates maintenance of stock registers, purchase records, and daily sale registers capturing customer name, address, technical name, batch number, quantity, and date. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L04** | **Permission for Online Booking & Digital Catalogs**: Verification of whether operating an online digital catalog and customer advance booking system for crop protection chemicals requires specific written endorsement from the District Joint Director of Agriculture (JDA) or licensing authority in Andhra Pradesh. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L05** | **Permission & Safety Rules for Home / Village Delivery**: Regulatory permissibility and transport safety requirements for doorstep or village delivery of toxic or hazardous agrochemicals (including spill containment, driver safety instructions, and transport prohibitions in passenger vehicles). | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L06** | **Restrictions on Specific Agrochemical Products**: Identification of restricted-use pesticides, state-specific seasonal bans, or scheduled hazardous chemicals under the Insecticides Act that may be prohibited from digital display, online booking, or retail sale in Andhra Pradesh. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L07** | **Required Label, Toxicity Triangle & Registration Display**: Compliance with Section 18 of the Insecticides Act, 1968 regarding packaging and labeling: mandatory display of CIB&RC registration numbers, toxicity hazard classification triangles (Red, Yellow, Blue, Green), approved antidotes, batch numbers, and manufacturing/expiry dates on digital catalog entries and customer receipts. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L08** | **GST Invoicing & Tax Slabs for Pesticides**: Correct Goods & Services Tax (GST) classification and HSN codes for technical and formulated chemical pesticides (typically 18% GST). Formal requirements for retail tax invoices, serial numbering rules, B2C vs B2B invoicing thresholds, and composition scheme eligibility under Andhra Pradesh GST rules. | Chartered Accountant | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH ACCOUNTANT** |
| **L09** | **Replacement of Physical Registers with Electronic Records**: Legal validity of electronic database ledgers and software-generated reports during statutory surprise inspections by Insecticide Inspectors under Section 21 of the Insecticides Act, 1968, versus the statutory mandate to maintain physically signed, bound paper registers. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh & Chartered Accountant | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH AND ACCOUNTANT** |
| **L10** | **Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities)**: Compliance with the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011: strict prohibition of selling above printed Maximum Retail Price (MRP), and mandatory display of net weight/volume, manufacturer name and address, and consumer care details on digital listings. | Legal Counsel / Chartered Accountant | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH LEGAL COUNSEL AND ACCOUNTANT** |
| **L11** | **Customer Credit (Udhaar) Enforceability**: Documentation, acknowledgement signatures, and terms required on counter receipts to establish legal enforceability of outstanding credit balances under commercial contract laws. | Legal Counsel / Chartered Accountant | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH LEGAL COUNSEL AND ACCOUNTANT** |
