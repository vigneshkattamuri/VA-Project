# Statutory & Legal Verification Checklist: Vighnesh AgroCare

## Legal Disclaimer
> **IMPORTANT NOTICE**:
> This document does **not** state legal conclusions, provide binding statutory interpretations, or substitute for formal legal and tax counsel. Agrochemicals, pesticides, plant growth regulators, and micronutrients are regulated commodities in India under the Insecticides Act, 1968, the Insecticides Rules, 1971, the Fertilizer Control Order, 1985, and Andhra Pradesh state agricultural regulations.
>
> Every item in this checklist must be reviewed, confirmed, and signed off by the business owner in consultation with the **Department of Agriculture (Government of Andhra Pradesh)**, a qualified **Lawyer**, or a licensed **Accountant** prior to commercial launch.

---

## Statutory Verification Checklist

| **L01** | **Insecticides & Pesticides Retail Sale Licence**: Validity of retail licence to sell, stock, or exhibit for sale insecticides under Section 13 of the Insecticides Act, 1968 and Rule 10 of the Insecticides Rules, 1971 in Andhra Pradesh. Verification of premises endorsement, qualified person certification, and valid principal supply certificates from manufacturers. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L02** | **Mandatory Display of Retail Licence**: Statutory requirement under Rule 10(1A) of the Insecticides Rules, 1971 to prominently display the insecticide retail licence on the shop premises, and verification of whether licence numbers must also be displayed across the digital PWA header, footer, and generated receipts. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L03** | **Mandatory Sale and Stock Records**: Compliance of digital inventory logs with Rule 15 of the Insecticides Rules, 1971, which mandates maintenance of stock registers, purchase records, and daily sale registers capturing customer name, address, technical name, batch number, quantity, and date. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L04** | **Permission for Online Booking & Digital Catalogs**: Verification of whether operating an online digital catalog and customer advance booking system for crop protection chemicals requires specific written endorsement from the District Joint Director of Agriculture (JDA) or licensing authority in Andhra Pradesh. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L05** | **Permission & Safety Rules for Home / Village Delivery**: Regulatory permissibility and transport safety requirements for doorstep or village delivery of toxic or hazardous agrochemicals (including spill containment, driver safety instructions, and transport prohibitions in passenger vehicles). | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L06** | **Restrictions on Specific Agrochemical Products**: Identification of restricted-use pesticides, state-specific seasonal bans, or scheduled hazardous chemicals under the Insecticides Act that may be prohibited from digital display, online booking, or retail sale in Andhra Pradesh. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L07** | **Required Label, Toxicity Triangle & Registration Display**: Compliance with Section 18 of the Insecticides Act, 1968 regarding packaging and labeling: mandatory display of CIB&RC registration numbers, toxicity hazard classification triangles (Red, Yellow, Blue, Green), approved antidotes, batch numbers, and manufacturing/expiry dates on digital catalog entries and customer receipts. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L08** | **Classification of Micronutrients & Plant Growth Regulators (FCO vs Insecticides Act)**: Verification of whether micronutrients and plant growth regulators (PGRs/bio-stimulants) fall under the Fertilizer Control Order, 1985 (FCO Schedule 1G / Biostimulant regulatory framework) or the Insecticides Act, 1968, and what specific retail licences, manufacturing source endorsements (e.g. Form O / Form A2), and statutory sales/stock registers apply in Andhra Pradesh. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L09** | **GST Invoicing & Tax Rates for Agrochemicals**: Applicable Goods & Services Tax (GST) slabs, HSN codes, and tax rates across product categories: chemical pesticides (typically 18%), plant growth regulators, and micronutrients. Statutory requirements for retail tax invoices, serial numbering rules, customer details capture, B2C vs B2B invoicing thresholds, and composition scheme rules under Andhra Pradesh GST. | Accountant | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH ACCOUNTANT** |
| **L10** | **Replacement of Physical Registers with Electronic Records**: Legal validity of electronic database ledgers, automated movement tables, and software-generated export reports during statutory surprise inspections by Insecticide Inspectors or Agricultural Officers under Section 21 of the Insecticides Act, 1968 and Fertilizer Control Order, versus the statutory mandate to maintain physically signed, bound paper registers on shop premises. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh, Accountant or Lawyer | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH, ACCOUNTANT or LAWYER** |
| **L11** | **Customer PII Storage & Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023**: Statutory obligations under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) for capturing, storing, and processing customer personal identifiers (customer name, mobile number, purchase history) on booking forms, sales receipts, and optional WhatsApp notifications: mandatory privacy notices at collection, purpose specification, storage limitation, data security measures, and customer rights. | Lawyer | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH LAWYER** |
| **L12** | **Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities)**: Compliance with the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011: strict prohibition of selling above printed Maximum Retail Price (MRP), and mandatory display of net weight/volume, manufacturer name and address, and consumer care details on digital listings. | Lawyer or Accountant | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH LAWYER or ACCOUNTANT** |
| **L13** | **Customer Credit (Udhaar) Enforceability**: Documentation, acknowledgement signatures, and terms required on counter receipts to establish legal enforceability of outstanding credit balances under commercial contract laws. | Lawyer or Accountant | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH LAWYER or ACCOUNTANT** |
