# Statutory & Legal Verification Checklist: Vighnesh AgroCare

## Legal Disclaimer
> **IMPORTANT NOTICE**:
> This document does **not** state legal conclusions, provide binding statutory interpretations, or substitute for formal legal and tax counsel. Agricultural inputs (fertilizers, pesticides, insecticides, and seeds) are strictly regulated commodities in India under central and state statutes.
>
> Every item in this checklist must be reviewed, confirmed, and signed off by the business owner in consultation with the **Department of Agriculture (Government of Andhra Pradesh)** and a licensed **Chartered Accountant** prior to commercial launch.

---

## Statutory Verification Checklist

| Item ID | Category | Statutory Area & Regulatory Context | Verification Authority | Verification Status |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **L01** | **Fertilizer Dealership License** | Valid Retail Dealership License under the Fertilizer (Inorganic, Organic or Mixed) (Control) Order, 1985 (FCO), issued by the Notified Authority in Andhra Pradesh. Required physical display of Form A/B/O and principal certificates from manufacturers. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L02** | **Insecticides & Pesticides License** | Retail license to sell, stock, or exhibit for sale insecticides/pesticides under Section 13 of the Insecticides Act, 1968, and Rule 10 of the Insecticides Rules, 1971. Verification of premises endorsement, qualified person certification, and principal supply certificates. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L03** | **Seeds Dealership License** | Retail dealer license/registration under the Seeds Act, 1966, and the Seeds (Control) Order, 1983, governing certified and truthfully labelled seeds in Andhra Pradesh. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L04** | **Legality of Online Booking & Digital Catalogs** | Verification of whether operating an online digital catalog and customer advance booking system for chemical fertilizers and insecticides requires special retail endorsement, prior intimation to the District Joint Director of Agriculture (JDA), or explicit written clearance. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L05** | **Home / Farm Gate Delivery of Agrochemicals** | Legal permissibility and transport safety requirements for doorstep or village delivery of toxic, hazardous, or restricted-use chemical pesticides and fertilizers (e.g., transport in passenger vehicles, spill containment protocols, driver safety precautions). | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L06** | **Product Labeling & Registration Numbers Display** | Legal obligation to display Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIB&RC) registration numbers, toxicity hazard classification triangles (Red, Yellow, Blue, Green), antidote instructions, batch details, and manufacturing/expiry dates on the digital catalog and online receipts. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L07** | **GST Invoicing & Tax Slabs** | Accurate Goods & Services Tax (GST) classification and HSN codes: chemical fertilizers (typically 5%), technical/formulated pesticides and insecticides (typically 18%), certified seeds (typically Nil/exempt). Format of retail tax invoices, serial numbering rules, B2C vs B2B invoicing thresholds, and composition scheme eligibility. | Chartered Accountant | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH ACCOUNTANT** |
| **L08** | **E-Way Bill Thresholds & Movement Rules** | Applicability and state-specific value thresholds for E-Way Bills during local farm delivery or godown-to-shop transport of goods in Andhra Pradesh. | Chartered Accountant | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH ACCOUNTANT** |
| **L09** | **Statutory Stock Registers & Inspection Records** | Legal compliance of digital electronic inventory logs versus mandatory physical bound stock and sale registers (e.g. Daily Sales Register, Form N under FCO 1985, Insecticide Inspection Register). Verification of whether electronic software prints are accepted as valid legal records during agricultural officer surprise inspections. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh & Chartered Accountant | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH AND ACCOUNTANT** |
| **L10** | **Subsidized Fertilizer Rules (PoS / Aadhaar)** | Rules regarding direct retail sales of non-subsidized commercial inputs versus subsidized fertilizers (e.g., Urea, DAP, MOP requiring Government of India Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) and biometric Aadhaar PoS devices). Clarification that the app only processes commercial/non-DBT retail or adheres to statutory DBT restrictions. | Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, ANDHRA PRADESH** |
| **L11** | **Customer Credit (Udhaar) Legal Enforceability** | Documentation, receipt acknowledgement signatures, and promissory terms required on counter receipts to establish legal enforceability of outstanding credit balances under Indian commercial contract laws. | Legal Counsel / Chartered Accountant | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH LEGAL COUNSEL AND ACCOUNTANT** |
| **L12** | **Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities)** | Enforcement of Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011: prohibition of selling above Maximum Retail Price (MRP), mandatory display of net weight/volume, manufacturer address, and consumer care details on digital listings. | Legal Counsel / Accountant | **TO BE VERIFIED WITH LEGAL COUNSEL AND ACCOUNTANT** |
