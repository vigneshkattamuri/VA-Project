# Statutory & Legal Verification Checklist: Vighnesh AgroCare

## Legal Disclaimer
> **IMPORTANT NOTICE**:
> This document does **not** state legal conclusions, provide binding statutory interpretations, or substitute for formal legal and tax counsel. Agrochemicals, pesticides, plant growth regulators, and micronutrients are regulated commodities in India under the Insecticides Act, the Insecticides Rules, the Fertilizer Control Order, and Andhra Pradesh state agricultural regulations.
>
> Every item in this checklist is formulated as an open question to be reviewed, confirmed, and signed off by the business owner in consultation with the **Department of Agriculture (Government of Andhra Pradesh)**, a qualified **Lawyer**, or a licensed **Accountant** prior to commercial launch.

## Action Items Prior to Production Go-Live
1. Consult the appointed **Accountant** to verify applicable pesticide, PGR, and micronutrient HSN codes, GST tax rates, invoice templates, and credit ledger documentation.
2. Submit formal queries to the **Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh** with retail licence copies to verify digital booking, delivery, electronic record-keeping plans, and regulatory classifications for PGRs and micronutrients.
3. Consult a **Lawyer** regarding customer personal data collection obligations under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, terms of service, and privacy notices.
4. Archive all verified dealership licences, registration credentials, and tax certificates in the store administrative records archive.
